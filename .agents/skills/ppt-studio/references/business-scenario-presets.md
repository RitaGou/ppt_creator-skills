# Business scenario presets

Use a preset to structure the story, not to invent information, page count, charts, or a visual theme. A user-designated PPTX/POTX reference overrides every preset's visual suggestions.

| Preset | Communication job | Typical cumulative story | Useful slide roles | Asset emphasis | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quarterly business review | Let leadership decide what to sustain, correct, and resource | conclusion → performance → causes → risks → next-quarter actions | executive takeaway, target versus actual, driver, risk, action owner | restrained KPI charts, approved team/product photo | Do not manufacture metrics or causal claims. |
| Client solution proposal | Earn confidence in a specific solution and next step | customer context → problem → solution → proof → delivery path → ask | client challenge, solution architecture, outcome, implementation, commercial next step | official product screenshots, solution diagram, authentic client-approved logos | Never imply a client endorsement without evidence. |
| Project review | Reach agreement on status, trade-offs, and recovery plan | goal → delivery status → evidence → blockers → decision → plan | goal, milestone, status, issue, decision request, next sprint | milestone timeline, risk matrix, diagrams | Separate facts from estimates and assumptions. |
| Strategy and roadmap | Align on direction and staged choices | strategic context → choices → recommendation → roadmap → dependencies → decisions | strategic thesis, option comparison, target state, phases, investment asks | scenario diagram, roadmap, minimal supporting illustration | Keep choices mutually understandable; do not disguise assumptions as commitments. |
| Product launch | Align teams around customer value and readiness | customer need → value proposition → launch scope → go-to-market → readiness → next step | problem, solution, target user, launch plan, readiness, owner | real product captures, approved product imagery, launch visual | Real product UI must be a real capture or clearly labelled concept. |
| Employee training | Help people perform a task safely and consistently | why → learning objective → method → practice → checklist → escalation | goal, principle, step-by-step procedure, example, checklist, contact | consistent instructional icons, real approved workplace images | Instructions must come from supplied policy or a named owner. |
| Marketing plan | Secure agreement on audience, message, channels, and measurement | objective → audience insight → message → campaign system → calendar → KPI plan | audience, value proposition, creative territory, channel plan, measurement | approved campaign imagery, moodboard only with rights metadata | Do not claim audience research not in evidence. |
| Employer brand / recruiting | Explain why the opportunity and culture fit candidates | mission → role impact → team → growth → process → call to action | company mission, role, team story, growth, hiring process | authentic employee/workplace media, official branding | Use real people only with approval. |
| Investor or partner pitch | Build conviction in opportunity, traction, and next step | headline → problem → solution → market → traction → model → ask | thesis, market, product, traction, economics, ask | source-backed charts, product images, simple diagrams | Every market/traction claim needs a source note. |
| Research brief | Make a conclusion and its evidence easy to challenge or accept | question → conclusion → method → evidence → implications → next research | conclusion, method, findings, evidence, limitations, recommendation | clean charts/tables, evidence diagram | Label confidence, limitations, and unknowns. |

## Custom narrative

When no preset fits, write one sentence in this form before outlining:

> For [audience], establish [decision or behavior] by showing [key evidence] and ending with [specific next step].

Then use the smallest set of slide roles that completes that argument.
